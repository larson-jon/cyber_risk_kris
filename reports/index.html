About these reports

  • KEV (Known Exploited Vulnerabilities) — CISA's catalog of CVEs confirmed exploited in the wild.
  • CVE / NVD — the full catalog of known vulnerabilities, scored for severity (CVSS) by NIST.
  • 8-K Item 1.05 — the SEC's mandatory disclosure of a material cybersecurity incident.
  • Data is collected by the collectors/ package; reports are generated by the analysis/ scripts. See the repository README for details.
